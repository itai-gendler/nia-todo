# nia-todo

nia-todo is a self-hosted todo app that keeps personal and shared work in one place on a server its owner runs. People organize todos in workspaces, projects, subprojects and sections; give them priorities, deadlines, repeats, reminders, subtasks, comments and attachments; and share individual projects with other people on the server. The server serves an installable, offline-capable web app, and the desktop (Windows, Debian) and Android apps connect to it.

## Intent

"Your tasks. Your server. Your data." A modern, self-hosted todo app with offline support, collaboration, native clients, and no external service dependency — keeping personal and shared work in one place while leaving its owner in control of the infrastructure.
